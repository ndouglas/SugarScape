## Source summary

- **Population and play:** "a period consists of each of 256 agents in the population selecting four others to play iterated Prisoner's Dilemma (IPD) games. An IPD game consists of four moves"; payoffs (Table 1) R 3, S 0, T 5, P 1. Strategy (y, p, q): y cooperates on the first move, p "following a cooperative move by the agent with which it is playing", q "after the other agent has defected". "We initialize y as equal to p, but they may vary independently thereafter." Payoffs accumulate over "all games X played during the current period" and are normalized to an "average payoff per move".
- **Start:** Appendix: "the initial population is generated by evenly distributing the agents throughout the strategy space"; §3.1: "starting from strategies that were initialized randomly". Fig. 1: in the first period "each of the four cells of the Prisoner's Dilemma matrix is realized one quarter of the time, making average payoff 2.25", then all structures decline.
- **Adaptation:** "Each agent A identifies the most successful of its neighbors (i.e. the agents A played in the current period) … Ties are broken randomly … adopting X's strategy if X did strictly better than A". "There is a 10 percent chance the agent makes a mistake when deciding whether or not its best neighbor did better than itself." Appendix: "regardless of which of the two strategies (its own or its best neighbors) is adopted, for each of the three parameters (y,p,q) there is a 10 percent chance that Gaussian noise is added to the parameter (mean 0, s.d. 0.4, bounded by 0 and 1)"; §2: "Agents also make errors in the actual copying process … during copying".
- **Structures (Appendix):** RWR — "four others chosen with equiprobability from the entire population (not including A itself) … with replacement", fresh each period. 2DK — "placed at random on a 16 by 16 torus. Each period each agent plays its 4 NEWS … neighbors … playing each twice during a period". FRN — "each agent A picks 4 other agents to be its neighbors, chosen with equiprobability and with replacement … retained for the entire run". FRNE — "each agent has exactly 4 other agents as (symmetric) neighbors … chosen at random … After all agents have 4 symmetric neighbors, each agent randomly swaps a neighbor with 256 randomly chosen other agents, with each swap subject to the FRNE constraints that each agent have 4 unique neighbors and each neighbor relation is symmetric." FFR-x — "in each period a new randomly chosen partner was substituted (temporarily, as in the RWR case) for each fixed network partner with probability" x.
- **Measures (Table 2; 30 runs of 2500 periods):** "Attain High C" — proportion of runs that "achieved a high level of cooperation at any time"; "Mean Payoff" — "average score per move … over the last 1000 periods"; "Remain High" — "proportion of time the populations remain at high levels of cooperation once they achieve it". The threshold for "high" is not stated.

| Structure | Attain high C | Mean payoff | Remain high |
|---|---|---|---|
| RWR | 0.30 | 1.091 | 0.015 |
| 2DK | 1.00 | 2.557 | 0.997 |
| FRNE | 1.00 | 2.575 | 0.995 |
| FRN | 1.00 | 2.480 | 0.942 |
| FFR 0.1 | 1.00 | 2.385 | 0.844 |
| FFR 0.3 | 1.00 | 2.100 | 0.402 |
| FFR 0.5 | 0.93 | 1.257 | 0.061 |

- **Mechanism claims:** in the region p ∈ [0.30, 0.35], q ∈ [0.05, 0.10] (population averages) the average p moves by −0.016 under RWR and +0.052 under FRN; the regression of partners' average p on own p there is "not significant for RWR" (7168 points) and "highly significant for FRN (slope = 0.1580; F = 717.20; N = 5376)"; FFR-0.3 is "bi-stable"; "Around a parameter value of 0.3 the dynamics shift … at levels of 0.5 and above, it collapses."
- **Notes:** note 1 — up to 4096 agents "display very similar aggregate statistics"; note 5 — "FRNE populations actually have a better average score than the 2DK populations"; Table A1 — FRNE agents at distance d = 1…6: 4.00, 11.74, 32.38, 74.59, 98.42, 33.23.

## Config

| Field | Default | Apply | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `agents` | 256 | reset | n (4–4096); a perfect square under `torus` |
| `structure` | `rwr` | reset | `rwr`, `torus` (2DK), `frne`, `frn` |
| `substitution` | 0 | live | x: each fixed link replaced for this period by a uniform random partner (not A) with probability x (FFR-x with `frn`; a switch the paper does not test with `torus`/`frne`; ignored under `rwr`) |
| `partners` | 4 | reset | partners each agent chooses (the torus's NEWS count is fixed at 4; `partners` then must be 4) |
| `moves` | 4 | live | moves per game |
| `judge_error` | 0.1 | live | chance the copy decision is inverted |
| `mutation` | 0.1 | live | chance per strategy parameter of noise |
| `mutation_sd` | 0.4 | live | the noise's standard deviation |
| `noise_on` | `always` | live | `always` (Appendix: every agent, every period) or `copy` (§2: only an agent that copied) |
| `start` | `grid` | reset | `grid` (Appendix: p and q on an evenly spaced grid, (i + ½)/k for k = ⌈√n⌉ values each, the first n points in row order; y = p) or `random` (§3.1: p, q uniform; y = p) |
| `high` | chosen in planning | live | "high cooperation": population mean payoff per move at least this (the paper does not state its threshold; planning measures the payoff distributions and records the choice) |
| `stop_at` | 0 | live | `finished()` at this period (0: never); presets set 2500 |

## Step (one period)

1. **Partners.** For each agent A, its chosen list: `rwr` — `partners` uniform draws with replacement from the others; fixed structures — its fixed list, each entry replaced (this period only) by a uniform random other with probability `substitution`. A plays one game with each entry; the other agent in each game is also credited.
2. **Game.** `moves` moves; on the first each cooperates with probability y; afterwards with p if the other cooperated on the previous move, else q. Both accumulate payoffs and move counts.
3. **Scores.** Each agent's score is total payoff ÷ total moves (0 for an agent that played nothing).
4. **Adaptation** (from the period's population, applied simultaneously): the "played" set of A is every distinct agent it met in either role; the best is the highest score there, ties uniform. Decide to copy iff best's score > A's score, inverted with probability `judge_error`. The next strategy is best's or A's own; then, if `noise_on` is `always` or A copied, each of y, p, q independently with probability `mutation` gets N(0, `mutation_sd`) noise, clamped to [0, 1]. An agent that played nobody keeps its strategy (and is still mutated under `always`).

Random draws are in a fixed order (partners, then games in agent order, then adaptation in agent order) so runs are deterministic.

## Fixed structures (at reset)

- `torus`: a random permutation places agents on the √n × √n torus; neighbors are the 4 NEWS sites. Each agent's chosen list is its 4 neighbors, so every pair plays twice.
- `frn`: each agent draws `partners` others uniformly with replacement.
- `frne`: a random `partners`-regular simple symmetric graph, built by pairing stubs at random (retrying on self-loops or duplicates), then the Appendix's mixing — each agent performs n random swap attempts (a double-edge swap with a random other edge, kept only if it creates no self-loop or duplicate). Each agent's chosen list is its neighbors, so every pair plays twice, as in 2DK. (CRA's swap procedure is described only in outline; this construction is a stated choice.) `agents × partners` must be even.

## Statistics

`SERIES`: `mean_payoff` (per move, whole population), `cooperation` (share of moves that cooperated), `mean_y`, `mean_p`, `mean_q`, `high` (1 if `mean_payoff` ≥ `high`), `attained_high` (first period that was high, else −1), `share_high_since` (share of periods from `attained_high` on that were high; 0 before), `copied` (share of agents that copied), `partner_p_slope` (least-squares slope of each agent's partners' mean p on its own p, this period).

Table 2's measures are read from these: Attain High C = whether `attained_high` ≥ 0 by period 2500; Mean Payoff = mean of `mean_payoff` over periods 1501–2500; Remain High = `share_high_since` at 2500.

## Views

- **Agents** (left): a √n-side block (⌈√n⌉ for non-squares), one cell per agent — under `torus` its lattice site, otherwise its index in row order (the help says so).
- **p–q plane** (right, 101 × 101 cells, p along the bottom, q up; a 6-cell gap between): the population's average (p, q) over the last 200 periods as a fading trail, and every agent as a dot (brighter where several coincide).
- **Color modes:** **Friendliness** (p), **Provocability** (1 − q), **Payoff** (this period's score), **Strategy** (nearest of TFT (1,1,0), ALLD (0,0,0), ALLC (1,1,1), or other when farther than 0.5 from all).
- **Inspect:** an agent: y, p, q, score, the partners it played this period (id, p, score, how many games), whether and whom it copied; a plane cell: its (p, q) and the agents there. `locate` returns an agent's block cell; Follow available.
- **Charts:** Mean payoff (with the threshold as a reference line); Cooperation; Strategy (p, q, y); High cooperation (`high`, `share_high_since`); Copying (`copied`, `partner_p_slope`). Time axis: Periods.

## Experiments and CLI

Seeds and periods measured to fit a browser run and recorded in each description; the survey runs the paper's 30 × 2500.
- `cra-table-2`: mean payoff over periods 1501–2500 per structure (x: the seven Table 2 rows).
- `cra-dial`: the same against substitution 0 … 1 in 0.1 steps (FRN).

- `cra-population`: mean payoff against n = 64 … 4096, series RWR and FRN.

## Survey

A `structure` claims module, 30 seeds of 2500 periods:
- Table 2: each row's attain-high, mean payoff (within a stated tolerance) and remain-high; the ordering RWR < FFR-0.5 < FFR-0.3 < FFR-0.1 < FRN ≈ 2DK ≈ FRNE; the shift "around 0.3" and collapse "at 0.5 and above".
- Fig. 1: first-period mean payoff ≈ 2.25; a decline in every structure; the fixed structures recover, RWR does not.
- The crucial region: average change of `mean_p` in periods whose population means lie in p ∈ [0.30, 0.35], q ∈ [0.05, 0.10] — negative under RWR (−0.016), positive under FRN (+0.052).
- Figs. 5–6: the partner-p slope in that region — ≈ 0.158 and significant for FRN, not for RWR.
- Note 5: FRNE's mean payoff above 2DK's; note 1: 4096 agents like 256; Table A1: FRNE fan-out at d = 1…6.
- The switches: how `start` and `noise_on` change the Table 2 rows.

Claims that fail are reported, and the descriptions and README say so.
