## Interpretation limits

Precision can resolve conditional numerical compatibility and model-mechanism contrasts; it cannot recover an absent original executable, RNG or statistic identity. A reading that matches the source is not thereby historically correct. Democratic nonattack is partly stipulated; successful selection/clustering does not establish learned cooperation or historical causality. Treat rare/extinction-conditioned outcomes and small differences honestly even with 100 histories; more bootstrap draws do not create independent evidence.
