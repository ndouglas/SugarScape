## Architectural recommendation for discussion

Prefer a separate `democratic_peace` kind with narrow reuse of neutral geopolitical helpers and existing hosts. The primary2001 dynamics have a different initialization, resource timing and combat consequence from the existing GeoSim2003 engine. A kind boundary makes the source clock and regime/alliance state explicit.

Three possible scopes:

1. **Separate2001 kind, recommended:** reproduce tagging, alliances and collective security with the paper's own outcomes, named readings and figure targets. More engine work than a flag extension, but a clear source contract.
2. **Environment enum inside GeoSim:** preserves one family label, but requires replacing much of its world state and phase pipeline. Potentially valid after a careful interface design; currently not a config-only extension.
3. **Combined2001/2004 family now:** adds contextual regime change and two source contracts at once. This expands measurement, calibration and interpretation work before the2001 ambiguities are settled.

The initial milestone should address2001. Cederman/Rao2001 supplies empirical context; Cederman/Gleditsch2004 supplies a later mechanistic challenge and possible separate environment. Neither is silently merged into the original defaults or acceptance criteria.
