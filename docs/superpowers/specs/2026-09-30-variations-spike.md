## What the episode can say

| Who | What they changed | What happens here | Survey |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pancs & Vriend 2007 | anyone moves, any time, to the square they like best; four kinds of wishes | Schelling's wishes: two clusters in 17 of 20; liking a mix up to half: 19 of 20; liking half and half best: about 5 clusters; the ring: two groups, 20 of 20 | hold |
| … their footnote | "spiked" (only a perfect mix will do) is "very similar" to p100 | 7.0 clusters, about as mixed as chance, against p100's 4.7 | fails |
| Gauvin, Vannimenus & Nadal 2009 | anyone, content or not, moves to a random square that suits; tolerance is like temperature | frozen at a tolerance of 0.3 (nobody can move), two clusters at ½, mixed at 0.8 | hold |
| Singh, Vainchtein & Weiss 2009 | a torus, 3 of 8 alike with empty squares counting against, a perfect mix with the empty squares removed | two clusters on 8 × 8, about 58 on 100 × 100: "strictly a small city phenomenon" | hold |
| Zhang 2004 (JEBO) | no empty houses; neighbors trade homes; everyone likes half and half best | from a perfect mix, mixed pairs fall from 20,000 to about 2,000, and stay | holds |
| … his Fig. 8 | waiting times | his cutoff (600 mixed pairs) is the least possible on his board; read as his scaled potential, reached 400 times sooner | fails |

The theme: change almost any rule and the town still sorts, unless tolerance runs high (Gauvin's
mixed phase) or nobody can move at all (frozen). Even wishing for a mixed street sorts it (Pancs &
Vriend's p50, Zhang, the ring). Size matters (Singh). Two of the papers' statements don't reproduce,
and the episode says so plainly and fairly (one beat), without guessing at why.

## How to film it

Schelling's felt board from episode 1, at each paper's own size: 5 × 5 up close (P&V), 50 × 50 and
100 × 100 from high, as the spatial-games episode filmed 99 × 99. The ring is Schelling's row from
episode 1, with a short arc of felt joining its ends (a new overlay, `ringjoin`). A small card names
each paper as its section starts (the Cooperation series' `source` card). No new frame dump: the
`schelling` and `line` shots carry every preset (swaps show as two Flumps walking past each other).

## Tune (proposed): *Variations on a Theme by Schelling*

A short theme in D on a music box (Schelling's), then one variation per paper, each in its own
voice. Pancs & Vriend: the theme on two clarinets that drift into unison. Gauvin: three moods: a
held chord that never moves (frozen), the theme split between a low and a high register
(segregated), both registers interleaved (mixed). Singh: the theme on a single fiddle, then the
full band playing it in scattered fragments. Zhang: a minuet where two voices trade phrases. The
misses: a bar of the theme with a wrong note, left unresolved. The title: the theme again, whole.
