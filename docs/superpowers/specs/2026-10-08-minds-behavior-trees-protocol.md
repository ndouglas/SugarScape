# Minds behavior-tree numerical protocol

This protocol declares collection and analysis. It records no prospective acceptance or scientific results. Engineering uses seeds 7 and 8. Registered execution remains on hold until separate actual prospective acceptance, after engineering gates and a frozen source/binary handoff. A tree is a representation and execution mechanism; supplied routines are not learned skills.

## Fixed budget and identities

Six controllers (reactive-utility, guarded-tree, matched-fsm, unguarded-tree, task-goap, legacy-goap), four scenarios (stable, better-alternative, depleted-target, temporary-obstacle), quotas 20 and 40, and two orientations form 96 cells. The exact 40 seeds are 30001 through 30040 inclusive, for 3,840 episodes. IDs are controller-scenario-quotaN-m0/m1. Reflection is x -> 10-x. Manifest schema is minds-behavior-tree-manifest-v1; raw core episodes use minds-behavior-tree-episode-v1; archive metadata uses minds-behavior-tree-archive-v1; measured analysis uses minds-behavior-tree-measured-v1. Synthetic scalar tests never claim the measured schema.

## Rig, clock, information and physical settlement

The grid is 11 by 11 with opaque boundary walls. Actor 1 begins at (2,5), holding 16, metabolism 1, vision 8, remembers true, age 0, maximum age 128. Walking speed is 1. No growback; carrying limit 0 means unlimited. Other ecological/social extensions are disabled by the exact checked rig.

There are 64 completed ticks and 65 raw frames including initial tick 0. Before action 3, better-alternative adds 12 to C; depleted-target drains the actual remaining B; temporary-obstacle closes food-free (5,5). That wall reopens before action 7. Stable does nothing. An occupied wall-event site is a fatal error; the event never moves. Event clocks continue after death. The action label equals the preceding completed tick plus 1. Memory Seen.tick uses the pre-increment core tick, so action 1 can record memory tick 0.

At most one physical attempt occurs in a live actor turn, including a failed attempt. Existing arrive gathers on an intervening site. Harvest settlement is mandatory independent of remaining traversal budget; actual gathering is never erased, RNG is never undone, and quota recognition is never postponed. Ordinary metabolism occurs exactly once afterward; death removes the actor but never ends the scene clock. Food balances include initial 52 site food plus 16 holdings, external additions/removals, nonnegative living holdings, actual consumption and death loss.

A selected site failing at action t is excluded from selection on t+1 and t+2, expiring at the start of t+3. Each deadline is independent; expiry also occurs on dead and hold ticks. Intervention changes do not clear deadlines. Pending no-eligible-target idle invokes ordinary arrive(origin), which may incidentally gather underfoot despite selection cooldown. It does not become a selected commitment and does not change a deadline. Completed-task holds gather zero, preserve the actual completing motion receipt/plan, and continue common metabolism until death or horizon.

Initial holdings do not count toward quota. Gross gathered determines first completion. Completion immediately clears retained task target, task plan, legacy plan and tree continuation. Valid unattained outcomes have restricted completion 64 and a recorded death-before-quota or horizon-without-quota reason. They remain in every denominator. A failed, invalid or incomplete episode is unavailable and cannot be substituted for a valid censored outcome.

Controller observations contain only the actor's ordinary visible candidates and remembered estimates in deterministic candidate order. No hidden true stock determines a remembered choice. Recorded Frame.observation is a separately identified pre-controller researcher projection; controllers rebuild, time and count their actual observations. It is not a free policy input. Raw visible values and remembered values are checked against pre-action state by saved-data validation.

The closed tree has at most 32 nodes, depth 8, and 64 node visits per actor tick; conditions are read-only, RNG-free and non-Running. There are no arbitrary scripts, parallel nodes, background workers or user graph input. Guarded and matched FSM policies retain eligible positive targets; unguarded omits only positive-value guarding, preserving candidate presence and cooldown eligibility. Selection uses ordinary utility with travel 1, crowding 0, idle stay. Task GOAP uses the remaining quota at planning time; a retained plan preserves that original goal. Legacy GOAP always plans for metabolism times the initial quota horizon. Both use existing reachable/visible-half-value retention, own slot plus eight rate-ranked other candidates, the existing Forage domain and 4096 search limit. Failed searches preserve unavailable exact expansion counts, never a guessed cap.

## Raw evidence, durability and provenance

Every archive is one writer and create-only. An immutable index.json precedes collection; each durable AttemptStart and empty JSONL stream precede construction. Files are flushed and synced, with parent directories synced. Each completed frame is appended and synced. An outcome receipt follows the final stream. final-index.json and census.json are new files, never rewrites of index.json. No retry, resume, overwrite, replacement seeds or favorable subset is supported. A hard I/O error stops at its actual durable prefix, retaining pending/unstarted counts and original files. Biological errors retain Failed outcomes and any known raw prefix. Torn final JSONL bytes are pending only without a conflicting completed outcome; conflicting well-formed records are invalid.

Attempt identities bind full frozen Git revision, committed raw protocol bytes, actual executable hash and mode, complete tracked-source inventory with modes, source inventory hash, exact manifest hash, toolchain/build flags and independently stamped compiled BT inputs. The BT closure includes every regular file under crates/sugarscape-core, survey/src and survey/build_support, excluding target, .git, .superpowers, node_modules and __pycache__ components, plus Cargo.toml, Cargo.lock, survey/Cargo.toml, survey/Cargo.lock, survey/build.rs, the approved design and this protocol. The SHA namespace is minds-behavior-tree-compiled-inputs-v1 followed by NUL, then sorted unique UTF-8 relative names, big-endian u64 name lengths, names, big-endian u64 byte lengths and actual bytes. Symlinks, nonregular inputs, duplicates and unsafe paths are rejected. Missing required source/locks are build errors. Absent protocol is omitted and stamped false; a present empty protocol includes its name/zero bytes and differs from absence. The generated Rust constants are included by the collector, which rejects stale same-path binaries before construction. After every durable start/empty stream and before each constructor, it rehashes the bound executable, protocol, complete tracked inventory and compiled-input closure and checks the Git revision. This extra I/O is outside both controller timing and the constructor-to-last-sink episode timer. An identity failure preserves that pending attempt and stops without retry. The separate prospective writer independently checks the expected frozen executable before invoking the collector; initial preflight does not invent a prior expected hash. This closure makes no claim that unrelated assets or generic dependencies were compiled; full Git inventory is separate.

All archive wire structures reject unknown fields. Nested positions reject all keys except integral x/y in the 11-grid before existing permissive Pos decoding. Raw frames retain tick, semantic fingerprint, canonical typed RNG JSON String, full actor traits/memory/motion, cells, task/tree/FSM/task-plan state, legacy plan, researcher observation, physical receipt, work counters, optional controller time, conservation totals, living ticks and errors. Complete episode metadata retains all frames, exact identity, first completion, censoring/reason, gross, living ticks, survival and errors. RNG u128 values are directly typed-deserialized and reserialized; never transformed through a generic numeric JSON value, JavaScript number or Debug output.

## Reporting and failure gates

Four separately labeled families compare guarded minus reactive (primary), Task GOAP (secondary), unguarded (ablation), and Legacy GOAP (reference). Each has all 16 scenario/quota/orientation strata times four metrics: quota attained, restricted completion ticks, gross gathered, living ticks. Thus 4 by 64 equals 256 estimates, each retaining all exact 40 seed pairs, using the existing paired-summary Student t interval and positive/zero/negative counts. Quotas and orientations are never pooled. Unrestricted completion summaries are null when any partner is unattained, listing every unavailable seed and reason.

All 640 guarded/FSM comparisons check every physical/task frame and exact canonical RNG String separately. Physical projections include tick, full actor traits/memory/motion, cells, receipt, quota/gross/first completion/target/deadlines, living ticks and conservation; they exclude representation state, instrumentation, fingerprint and RNG. Physical duplicates and whole-condition aliases are reported; unique RNG-bearing fingerprints do not establish independence. Depleted-target/quota40 is a known floor, and related food/lifetime endpoints are not independent corroboration.

Per-invocation native timing spans the actual common controller wrapper, including live cooldown expiry, but excludes researcher projection, frame serialization, sink I/O and analysis. It is unavailable on WASM, holds and dead ticks, never fabricated zero. Active-call denominator counts turns entered alive with quota pending, including completion/death turns; hold-call denominator counts turns entered alive already complete. Each episode retains its invocation times and total (null if any active timing unavailable), actual work totals, active/hold calls and survival. Per-episode native wall timing spans constructor through final frame sink and includes raw encoding/sync, excluding outcome encoding/sync. These boundaries prevent survival and hold duration from being mistaken for executor speed.

Analysis requires the complete canonical valid archive and every declared partner. It stores raw references, all endpoints and estimates, per-cell distributions, unavailable reasons, physical duplicates/aliases and exact separate RNG identities. It emits no overall Holds/Fails, significance verdict or animal independence claim. Any provenance mismatch, stale binary, impossible raw action/state, noncanonical manifest, missing/duplicate partner or matched physical/RNG discrepancy is a failure gate. Prospective acceptance, registered collection, publication and evidence archival remain separate pending tasks.
