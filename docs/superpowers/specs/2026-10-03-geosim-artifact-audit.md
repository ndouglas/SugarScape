## Unrepresented certification blockers

- **Two streams and normal consumption:** `Model:153–158` creates two seed5 Colt MersenneTwisters. Every primitive constructor consumes superiority-normal, threshold-normal, then resource-Bernoulli before initial shuffle (`Actor.java:79–90`; `Model:177–190`). Even SD0 normals consume draws (`Model:1067–1068`). Every sovereign resource update also consumes a normal at SD0 (`Actor.java:375/377`). The portable engine uses one PCG and skips those draws. The second stream shuffles sovereigns after initialization and after claim events (`Model:200–201,840–854,1014–1017`), with ordering retained between those events.
- **Founder and resource state:** artifact IDs are `x*height+y`; portfolio candidates follow insertion order. Growth selects victim then path before conquest (`Model:235–249`), transfers full taxRate1 child resource and stores its parent distance (`610–634`). First resource update replaces newly-independent capacity and initializes old front commitments from pre-replacement resources (`Actor.java:357–377`). Sorted owner sets and summed capacities alone do not certify this state.
- **Shock timing:** artifact shocks precede resources for time>500 (`Model:973–989`), whereas paper reconstruction puts shocks after structural claims for the next period. Archived negative20 denotes extractive frontier shifts (`864–868,879–895`); portable positive20 is an attributed semantic translation.
- **Attack distance:** initiator's curve uses opponent target's own-parent stored distance, not initiator-capital→target distance (`Actor.java:675–682,287–291`; `Model:614`). Actual mutual battle uses respective owners' curves (`Model:566–568`). A config called initiator-curve does not prove its distance arguments are correct.
- **Victory and collision:** artifact defender uses reciprocal threshold `1/vict` on its own advantage (`Model:561–575`), matching APSR printed148. The initial approved spec and core snapshot mistakenly used the same positive threshold for both sides; this is a corrected paper-default error, not evidence of port-versus-paper divergence. A named reciprocal/same-threshold field is being coordinated before measurement; once implemented the reciprocal default represents this probability rule. Draw ordering/collision remain separate certification questions. Simultaneous new attacks resolve with an integer draw (`480–488`), not the paper's deterministic collision reading. Battle is mutual-D only; unilateral attacks cause damage/activity but no battle and no severity increment (`525–554`). The exposed stalemate parameter is unused (`Lab:173`).
- **Structural ordering:** claims execute in existing sovereign/portfolio order, with government checks and immediate relation/conquest mutations (`Model:808–832`), followed by second-stream shuffle. The port has no explicit generation-aware or affected-cell lock ledger. Absorption clears child war/campaign pointers; reemergence reuses Actor identity (`610–634`, ending712).
- **Shadows and merges:** GC consolidates after structural changes, then trims (`Model:1011–1021`; `War.java:234–254`). Absorbed current members receive refreshed full shadows (`War.java:103–108,171–179`). A maximum-last-fight timestamp merge does not represent this. Removal tests shadow<=0 before decrementing inactivity (`127–148`); atshadow0 even current fighters are removed in that GC. Nonsov removal and default20 expiry alone do not certify identity. No split occurs; absorbed historic-member/magnitude information is not wholly unioned.

## Primary-paper correction and damage-incidence ambiguity

A fresh read of `papers/geopolitics/cederman-2003-apsr-modeling-the-size-of-wars.pdf`, printed148/PDF15, corrects this audit's earlier characterization: reciprocal defender threshold is explicitly the paper rule. Define attacker advantage A; defender evaluates its own advantage1/A with threshold1/3, giving q_def=1/[1+(A/3)^20]. Defense is favored at equal resources and becomes less likely as attacker advantage increases. Artifact `Model:561–575` implements that orientation. The previous same-threshold snapshot remains evidence of what was inspected, not a valid primary-paper interpretation. Full certification stays Unresolved for the other mechanics; implementing the corrected named threshold alone will resolve this particular representational gap.

Printed148 charges attacking state j a cost .1*res(j,i), while printed147 TableA1 describes opponent-inflicted damage and the resource pseudocode subtracts sum damage(j,i); printed140 describes all parties' accumulated front damage. These are conflicting incidence/magnitude readings, not a resolved single formula. A separate unmeasured `DamageIncidence::{AttackedParty,ActingParty}` can express the distinction without changing current default or registered37arms. ActingParty+OwnCommitment implements literal148 acting state's raw local cost; default AttackedParty+OpponentProjected remains the attributed table/resource/port reading. `DamageBasis` alone changes the amount and cannot alter which C/D side pays. This source clarification introduces no measured tuning or registered workload expansion.
