## Reproducible evidence and limitations

`probe.cpp` is the standalone engine; seeds.csv and seeds_enriched.csv retain all50-seed outcomes per case. summary.csv retains conditional means, sample SD, quantiles and censoring. selected_trace.csv is prespecified seed2001; full traces are generated by rerunning. Inapplicable policy metrics for nonpolicy cases are N/A despite generic censored fields. Pre-switch99/100 nonattainment concerns the pre-switch observation window.

Other conventions: fixed-slot replacement, oldest cohorts first with internal shuffle, own-activation aging, mortality before retirement, newborn20, immediate network drawing, distinct existing birth-cohort peers, positive counted denominator, threshold equality. These are explicit reconstruction choices; Slot and cohort direction are not uniquely specified by the source. No groups or renewal alternatives are tested by this independent engine.

Native `previous_tick_mode_at_switch` is the rolling retirement-event mode from the tick immediately BEFORE the recorded switch tick. It excludes switching-tick events. Independent `mode_at_switch` includes events through the actual switch tick. These diagnostics have different observation windows and must not be compared directly. Native representative trace `modal_age` at the switch tick is a separate observation; no per-seed switch-tick values are inferred from the single retained trace.
