Using rival bids to update every action raises FPA terminal revenue to **.906800** (500/500 stable; SE .000767), versus .232000 in the matched chosen-action sample. Low bids, below-top profiles and profitable deviations are separate measures.

Constant exploration differs from a fixed terminal-policy experiment. The source's single seed per format completes 100m auctions: FPA whole realized revenue .225344 and SPA .622917; SPA whole top played-pair occupancy **.309875**, far below .80, with late occupancy .310061. The persistent check fails despite low FPA revenue. Twenty independent 10m sessions show SPA whole top occupancy .299037 (session SE .000834; [.297403,.300671]) and late .310764 (SE .002026), supporting the finite-trajectory observation beyond seed 1. Persistent FPA whole revenue is .241379 and SPA .617655; terminal stable counts are 19/20 and 1/20, but the occupancy/revenue summaries include every session. No uncertainty estimate treats millions of dependent periods as independent replications.

Under decaying exploration, all 20 duration seeds per format remain stable with unchanged terminal revenues at 1m/10m/100m: FPA .222500 and SPA .950000, paired horizon differences zero. SPA whole top occupancy rises from .716245 to .971537 to .997148 as the initial transient becomes a smaller fraction; late occupancy is already near one. Unchanged terminal policies and changing whole-run occupancy are compatible statistics. These finite runs do not decide an epsilon-to-zero stationary-distribution theorem, especially one assuming deterministic split ties and a grid without the weak .90 equilibrium.

## Sensitivity and interpretation limits

Initialization is a substantial reconstruction dependency. Across the 100-seed declared arms, FPA means span .446500; stage optimism gives FPA .676500/SPA .853000, constant Q=2 gives .667000/.803000, and Q=10 gives .354000/.586000. Thus the baseline low-FPA/high-SPA direction does not survive every initialization reading. Constant Q=100 agrees with discounted optimism on these seeds (.230000/.950000); Q=1,000 gives .235000/.944000. These arms stay descriptive and do not choose a new default.

Every covered sampled/expected actual-tie and greedy-tie pair retains the directional distinction, but FPA means span .189000. Expected rewards at actual ties produce about .05 FPA revenue, much lower than sampled reward arms around .214–.239. In feedback FPA, the four actual/hindsight tie combinations span .043000: sampled actual/realized hindsight gives .950000, while the expected-hindsight arms are about .907000. Tie noise and counterfactual conventions affect endpoints even when the qualitative intervention persists. Full arm tables follow in the registered records.

The safety connection is an interaction outcome: independent reward-seeking tabular bidders can benefit jointly while seller revenue falls, and feedback that the learner uses can change that outcome. The experiment provides no evidence of intent, communication, deception or learned punishment. Scalar bids, participation choices, history state and budget pacing are different environments. No original author simulator was located; ACM supplement access was unresolved, so neither exact code docking nor universal auction-format protection is established. The figure/prose discrepancies and undefined extension conventions remain explicit, even where a registered reading holds.
