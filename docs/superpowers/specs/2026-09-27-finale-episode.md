## Tune: "The Walk Home"

A new piece quoting the pilot's gånglåt theme ("Flumps' Walk") in D: each episode's instruments take
a phrase in turn (Seasons' violin, Pollution's brass, Inheritance's cello, Tribes' recorders,
Markets' piano, War's timpani, Credit's bass, Contagion's accordion), thinning to a lone accordion
for the ledger and ending on the pilot's opening bar.
