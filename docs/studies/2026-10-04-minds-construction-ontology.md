## Purpose and present position

Physical actions, material histories and agent information already have strong operational boundaries. Usefulness, cooperation, ownership, culture and institutions need equally explicit definitions as the programme grows.

## Things, relations, processes and interpretations

A worker, material unit, key or passage is an identifiable thing. Carrying, observing, trusting, claiming and being able to traverse are relations. Digging, communicating, learning and repairing are processes. Useful chamber, betrayal and sacred place are interpretations with additional prerequisites and measurements.

These categories are modeling aids, not a commitment to a universal philosophical ontology or a class hierarchy. A concept can involve several categories: a message has a persistent record, a production event, an audience relation and a receiver's interpretation. Prefer explicit links between these components over one field carrying an entire explanation.

| Question | Vocabulary |
|---|---|
| What physically exists? | Bodies, materials, objects, terrain and structures |
| What can happen? | Capabilities, affordances, action prerequisites, costs and consequences |
| What information is available? | Observations, memories, estimates, messages and uncertainty |
| What matters to whom? | Needs, preferences, objectives, benefits and harms |
| What social arrangements exist? | Membership, commitments, rights, obligations and authority |
| How does this persist or change? | Learning, transmission, inheritance, maintenance and abandonment |

Researcher truth and evidence accompany all six questions without silently entering an agent's information. A record, a visualization and an agent's observation can describe different projections of the same world.

## Embodiment, affordances and action

Represent an affordance through a relationship between an actor's capabilities, environmental conditions and an action. Passage dimensions, carried loads, tools, fatigue and occupants may alter that relationship. Keep physically possible, perceived possible, socially permitted, selected, attempted and successfully completed separate.

A passage may admit a kobold while excluding a larger orc. An agent may misjudge an opening it can use. A usable doorway may be forbidden. A key may enable entry while its holder lacks permission. These examples specify experiments rather than fixed traits of fantasy populations.

Warren's stair-climbing experiments provide an empirical anchor for evaluating action possibilities relative to an actor's body. Our lattice analogues simplify this relationship and do not reproduce biomechanics or the whole ecological theory of perception. [Warren (1984)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6238127/).

## Information and external memory

Use the following distinctions in campaign designs; refine contested terms through explicit operational definitions.

- **Cue:** an observable feature that informs action.
- **Message:** content produced and made available through a specified channel.
- **Claim:** an assertion about the world with a source and time.
- **Belief or estimate:** an agent's representation, which may be wrong.
- **Researcher truth:** independently inspectable experimental state.

A spoil pile can serve as a cue without being an addressed symbolic message. A stale note, mistaken report and deliberately misleading report can contain the same assertion while arising through different processes. Information designs should specify source, time, audience, access, persistence, reliability and production/reading costs. Hidden truth must not enter a recipient's controller through a diagnostic field.

Stigmergy, coordination through manipulation and observation of a shared environment, is a useful mechanism family. Termite-inspired robot construction provides a concrete example; its engineered local rules do not establish that our workers possess shared intentions. [Werfel, Petersen and Nagpal (2014)](https://ssr.princeton.edu/sites/g/files/toruqf2946/files/documents/science2014-termes.pdf).

For hive minds and private networks, independently specify shared information, shared objectives and shared decision authority. Channel membership alone does not establish encryption, a learned code or a single collective mind. Shared memory need not erase individual interests or responsibility.

## Motivation and social relations

Separate needs, utility/preference representations, supplied tasks and observed outcomes. Planning does not establish learning; remembered events do not establish a model of another agent's mind; an optimizing rule does not establish consciousness or welfare. Sophistication has several dimensions, each with computational and opportunity costs.

Possession, creation, control of access, ownership claims, recognized rights and enforcement should be independent. A cache creator ID is a mechanistic relation; a social ownership institution needs additional rules and consequences. An empty cache site may remain claimed. An agent may control an object it does not own.

- Helping requires a specified benefit to another agent.
- Costly helping additionally requires a specified cost to the helper; identify the outcome currency and horizon.
- Reciprocity requires a relationship between assistance across occasions.
- Defection requires a defined cooperative arrangement.
- Betrayal requires an identifiable commitment or expectation that was breached.

For altruism, distinguish behavioral cost/benefit measures from evolutionary fitness claims and proximate motivation. For deception, distinguish false outcomes from policies selected to induce a recipient's mistaken estimate. Choose measurable definitions before trials rather than assigning motives after outcomes.

Safety experiments should independently represent ability, permission, knowledge of restrictions, motivation and concealment. Separate the researcher's observations from an overseer's available information. Institutional rules can regulate participation, information, choices and allocation; Ostrom's institutional framework is a research anchor, not validation of a particular proposed agent society. [Ostrom's Nobel lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/ostrom_lecture.pdf).

## Places, architecture and history

An excavated cavity initially has geometry. Store, home, shrine and council chamber describe activities, users and social arrangements that require their own evidence. A named chamber is not automatically functional.

Trace construction, access, use, maintenance, reuse, abandonment and destruction. A space may serve multiple functions or be contested. An abandoned shrine might become a store while remaining sacred to some inhabitants. Infrastructure carries history even when no inhabitant remembers its construction.

Keep three uses of architectural patterns distinct: a requirement supplied to a generator, a regularity measured in layouts and a practice learned by inhabitants. Alexander's patterns provide candidate relationships between requirements and form; the construction programme preserves their evidentiary limits. A generated settlement does not establish that inhabitants designed it. A layout produced by local construction does not establish that agents held a blueprint.

## Culture and environmental interpretation

Configured preferences, biologically inherited strategies, copied practices, conventions and enforced norms are different mechanisms. An initial operational tradition can be a practice spreading through social learning and persisting beyond its original demonstrators. Aplin and colleagues provide an experimental anchor in socially transmitted, persistent foraging variants in wild great tits. [Aplin et al. (2015)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4344839/).

Vary inherited infrastructure separately from transmitted practices. Later cohorts may follow an ancestral tunnel because it exists, because they learned its utility, or because a prohibition restricts excavation. Migration, biological inheritance and cohort replacement need distinct rules.

For astronomical influences, investigate intermediate links: physical cycles, observable cues, ecological consequences, learned predictions, calendars, conventions and institutions. A binary sun does not alone explain a religion or language. Distinguish physical causation from cultural interpretation and public coordination.

A fantasy population is a configured combination of bodies, ecology, learning opportunities, institutions and history. Hold bodies constant while varying institutions, and institutions constant while varying environment. Avoid making a species name an explanation of architecture or beliefs.

## Campaign ontology record

Add a compact record for consequential concepts to future designs. Use prose or a table matching the design; no new serialization system is required.

| Field | Required question |
|---|---|
| Definition and category | What exactly does the concept mean here: entity, relation, process or interpretation? |
| State carrier | Where does its state live, and what identities does it depend on? |
| Visibility | Which agents or observers can inspect which parts, through what mechanism? |
| Change mechanism | Which events create, update or remove it, at what cost? |
| Measurement | What observable distinguishes its presence, success or failure? |
| Origin and evidence | Is it supplied, discovered, learned, negotiated or emergent? What evidence supports which claim? |

Distinguish verified formal mechanisms, measured model behavior, empirical regularities, published-model reproduction, speculative compositions and aesthetic goals. Evidence for an atom does not automatically validate its combination with other atoms.

Useful boundary cases include a passable but forbidden door; a held key without entry rights; a stale truthful-at-writing note; an empty but claimed store; a structurally open route no consumer has visited; and a repaired room whose practice was copied rather than genetically inherited.

## Dependency map and next steps

Keep the approved resource-access increment first. It separates a supplied coordinate, private observation of completion and the researcher's structural milestone. Its [ontology record](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-04-burrow-2-resource-access-design.md#ontology-record) makes those boundaries explicit.

1. **Resource access:** preserve task-information controls, local completion memory and structural measurements.
2. **Inhabitation and benefit:** add extraction, delivery, consumption or shelter with explicit beneficiaries and costs.
3. **Embodiment and controls:** introduce capability variation, gates and tools; distinguish opportunity, perception and permission.
4. **Communication and commitments:** introduce provenance, audiences, persistence and costs, then assistance, promises and strategic information.
5. **Maintenance and transmission:** distinguish repair, replacement cohorts, learned practices and inherited infrastructure.
6. **Institutions and integration:** combine authority, rights, oversight and historical change once the component mechanisms are understood.

Resource access is now merged. The [foraging/construction draft](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-04-foraging-construction-design.md) refines the next inhabitation/benefit step into a published-rule reference, fixed-world reconstruction, passage adaptation and construction coupling. Its [source audit](2026-10-04-foraging-construction-reading.md) distinguishes CPFA's private site fidelity and server-mediated waypoints from chemical signaling and unrestricted shared knowledge. The written draft awaits review; its existence does not approve runtime work or scientific execution.

This is a dependency guide, not a replacement for the existing C1–C10 or A–G programme labels or approval of their implementations. Minds protection, deception and decision-engine comparisons retain their places alongside construction. Hornvale interfaces still need inspection before an integration contract is assigned.

The major bridges still to cover are needs/motivation, calibration through experience, social learning, commitments/institutions and the lifecycle of constructed places. Use a shared conceptual vocabulary now; share runtime machinery when physical, information, timing, cost and evidentiary contracts have been shown compatible. A universal mind class or ontology engine is not a prerequisite.
