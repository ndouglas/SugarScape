## LinkedIn

Video: `sugarscape-001-sugar.mp4`

> In 1996, Joshua Epstein and Robert Axtell published *Growing Artificial Societies*: a grid of sugar,
> a few hundred simple agents, and a handful of rules. From those rules, crowds, migration, wealth
> inequality, inheritance and tribes all emerge.
>
> I've been rebuilding their model, along with a growing list of other agent-based models from the
> literature, as an open-source engine you can run in the browser. And I've been making short
> explainer films where every creature's every move comes from a real run.
>
> The rule for the films is that every caption is measured over 20 runs before it goes on screen.
> When the book's result doesn't hold up, the caption changes, not the data. The book says, for
> example, that trade turns a society that crashes into one that doubles. However we fill in the
> details the book leaves out, it never does in our runs.
>
> Ten episodes: Sugarscape, Seasons, Pollution, Inheritance, Tribes, Markets, War, Credit, Contagion,
> and a finale on what didn't reproduce.
>
> Try the model: ndouglas.github.io/SugarScape
