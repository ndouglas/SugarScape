## Support and density equations

All four alternatives use the same Pareto-selected **inclusive** support x>=m=xmin. Let y=log(x)-log(m), t=x/m, and k be a positive dimensionless rate.

- Pareto: f(x)=(alpha−1)/m * t^(−alpha), alpha>1; MLE alpha=1+1/mean(y).
- Exponential: f(x)=k/m * exp[−k(t−1)]; k=1/mean(expm1(y)), physical rate lambda=k/m.
- Lognormal: f(x)=NormalDensity(log(x);mu,sigma)/(x*NormalSurvival(log(m);mu,sigma)), sigma>0. Use natural coordinates s=mean(y), u=y/s, a=−(mu−log(m))*s/sigma², b=s²/(2sigma²)>0. Then f(x)=exp(−a*u−b*u²)/(x*s*J), J=integral0∞exp(−a*u−b*u²)du. Profile/converge using moment-gradient differences. mu=log(m)−a*s/(2b), sigma=s/sqrt(2b).
- Stretched exponential (Weibull): f(x)=beta*k/m * t^(beta−1)*exp[−k(t^beta−1)], beta>0; beta>1 is allowed and explicitly a compressed exponential. Profile k=n/sum(expm1(beta*y)); solve the one-dimensional score root.
- Exponential-cutoff Pareto: f(x)=t^(−alpha)*exp[−k(t−1)]/(m*J), J=integral0∞exp[(1−alpha)u−k*expm1(u)]du. For positive k, alpha may be any real value; at k=0 normalizability requires alpha>1. This dimensionless positive integral avoids SciPy incomplete-gamma functions at negative shape. Scale around its mode and remove coordinate scale outside quadrature.

Data-selected Pareto xmin minimizes KS on eligible observed cutoffs with at least50 inclusive tail observations. Individual histories use all observed unique eligible values; pools use every value up to100 or integer index `j*(M−1)//99`, j0..99, from the ascending M eligible values. Include endpoints/no duplicates. At each unique tied tail value use both left and right ECDF limits. Exact KS ties choose the smallest xmin. All-equal/insufficient/nonfinite inputs are explicit unavailable fits. A selected Pareto fit is descriptive when event data are dependent.

## Numerical starts, guards and failure semantics

Positive normalizer quadrature: SciPy `quad`, epsabs1e−11, epsrel1e−10, limit250. Reject warnings, nonpositive/nonfinite result/error and estimated relative error>1e−8. These are quadrature estimates, not certified error bounds. Mode-shift/scale integrands so the absolute tolerance applies to an order-one integral. Retain exact k0 normalizer.

Lognormal L-BFGS-B starts(a,b)=(-1,.5),(0,.5),(1,.1),(1,.01), guards a[-100,100], b[1e−8,1e4]. Scaled second moment>=2 means unattained Pareto-limit MLE, not a favorable fitted lognormal. Guard-contact tests match the public kernel. Cutoff starts alpha=[min(99,alphaPareto),1,0] crossed with k=[1,.1,.01]/mean(expm1(y)); logk starts clipped[-31,31], guards alpha[-100,100]/logk[-32,32]. Both optimizers use ftol1e−13, gtol1e−8, maxiter1000, maxls50; successful finite final objective with gradient infinity norm<1e−6 is required. Exact-NLL ties preserve start order. Exploration sentinels cannot become a likelihood. Guard bounds are numerical protection, not scientific restrictions; contacting them is Unresolved.

Weibull uses analytic profiled score, stable small-beta series/scaled exponential sums and brentq beta[1e−8,100] with upper doubling from1; xtol/rtol1e−10, maxiter200. Nonpositive endpoint score is an unattained Pareto limit. Guard contact/unconverged/underflowed zero rate is Unresolved. Evaluate the final k*(exp(beta*y)−1) as exp(logk+beta*y)−k to avoid an overflowing unscaled exponential.

Cutoff exact boundary: alphaPareto>2 and mean(expm1(y))>=1/(alphaPareto−2) supplies the convex likelihood KKT certificate for k0. Return equal Pareto likelihood/T0/p1. Interior gain<=1e−10 per observation is unresolved near-boundary, not manufactured equality. All failed/nonfinite final evaluations/parameters retain telemetry and Unresolved status. Null unavailable numeric diagnostic fields have explicit `unavailable_numeric_fields` paths; finite fitted numbers are never silently coerced.

Reference fixture tolerances: normalization relative<=1e−9, logdensity absolute<=1e−8, independent-optimizer parameter absolute<=2e−5, NLL absolute<=1e−9 per observation, tied KS absolute<=1e−12, alpha/KS rescaling absolute<=1e−10. These check numerical implementation, not equality to generating parameters. They were selected from synthetic/reference preparation before scientific measurement.

## Ratios and calibration qualification

Nonnested R=sum(log fPareto−log fAlternative); variance=mean((difference−mean difference)²), divisor n; normalized R=R/sqrt(n*variance), two-sided erfc p at.1. Nonfinite/variance<=1e−24 and unsuccessful alternative fits are Unresolved. Keep exponential/lognormal/Weibull/cutoff separately, including disagreement.

Declared nested cutoff calibration is half point mass0/half chi-square1; positive T=−2R gets p=.5*chi2.sf(T,1), exact KKT equality p1. Preserve `calibration=declared_half_chi_square_1`, **calibration_validity=unvalidated**. The boundary score contains X; under pure Pareto its first moment fails for alpha<=2 and second moment/Fisher information fails for alpha<=3. Alpha>3 is necessary for that moment condition, not validation of all regularity conditions, cutoff selection, dependence or finite-n calibration. The [Self–Liang boundary likelihood reference](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1987.10478472) does not justify silently applying regular finite-information asymptotics outside its assumptions. No new dependent-null or calibrated empirical S1 test is invented.

## Resampling and incomplete populations

22 fixed original/precision pools receive1000 generated/refitted iid KS replicates only when the whole arm is complete and fit eligible. Draw Binomial tail count, empirical-body indices, Pareto exponential logtail values in that fixed order; refit grid100/alpha in every replicate. `b=count(Dstar>=Dobs)`, p=(b+1)/(B+1),95% Clopper–Pearson from b/B without pseudo count. CI touching/straddling.1 is Inconclusive; wholly above is not rejected under iid diagnostic, wholly below rejected under iid diagnostic. Any predetermined failed replicate makes the test Unresolved; retain all attempts/failures without replacements. This is not treatment inference or dependent-history validation.

72 fixed NumPy PCG64 jobs derive independent children via `SeedSequence(2026100301,spawn_key=(job_index,))`: eleven joint source predictions, two joint source contrasts,22KS pools and37arm parameter bootstraps. Missing jobs retain indices. Source predictions draw15 joint precision histories; source contrasts independently draw100 rows per treatment/control group. Batch size1024 is fixed; base block precedes control block. Inclusive +1 two-tail p-values and closed source rounding interval maximization feed fixed88/6 Holm families with unavailable entries internally p1; adjusted p<.05 rejects. Linear95% quantiles and arithmetic medians are frozen.

Parameter100000 draws resample all registered history rows with joint Pareto alpha/xmin eligibility masks. Max-scaled positive means and arithmetic median midpoints with overflow-safe branch preserve finite large parameters. Any incomplete underlying population or zero-eligible replicate makes primary uncertainty Unresolved; optional conditional nonempty intervals are descriptive with exclusions. Base−shock0/context-off parameter contrasts subtract independent fixed per-arm replicate arrays at the same index, with descriptive95% intervals for alpha/xmin means/medians; no new RNG job, war bootstrap, optimizer refits or invented p-family. Primary invalid histories never enter pools/bootstraps. Raw completions after the successful clock are preserved but excluded from **every** fit, including partial diagnostics.
